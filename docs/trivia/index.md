# Trivia and Useless Facts

Not everything worth writing down is worth acting on. This section collects the
side-quests: numbers nobody asked for, historical detours, curiosities dug out
of the source, and experiments run purely because the question wouldn't leave me
alone.

Nothing here will fix your build or get your shop online. It may, however, give
you a better feel for the thing you are working with — or at least something to
say at the next meetup.

- [A Function Point Count of Shopware 6.7](function-point-analysis-of-shopware.md): How big is Shopware in Function Points (IFPUG, ISO 20926)? A complete count of 6.7.13.0 out of the box, and what the exercise taught me about the method.
